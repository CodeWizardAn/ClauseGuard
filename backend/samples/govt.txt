GOVERNMENT SCHEME CIRCULAR (sample — no personal details)

Subject: Student scholarship and hostel assistance — key conditions

1. Who can apply
Students from notified categories who are enrolled in a recognised college may apply. The family income ceiling is as notified each year.

2. Documents
Applicants must upload income proof, enrolment proof, and bank details of a student account. Original IDs should not be emailed. This sample does not collect names or numbers.

3. Deadlines
Applications close 30 days after the notice date. Late forms are rejected with no appeal except for a documented hospital stay.

4. What the money can be used for
The grant is only for tuition and hostel mess. Buying devices, travel, or coaching is not allowed. Misuse can lead to a 3-year bar from all state schemes.

5. Disbursal
Money is sent in two parts. The second part is paid only if attendance is above 75% and there is no backlog paper.

6. Recovery
If a student drops out mid-year, the full amount already paid must be returned within 45 days, with 12% yearly interest.

7. Changes
The department may change rules in the middle of the year. Students already approved are also bound by the new rules.

8. Grievance
Complaints must be filed on the portal within 15 days. After that, no court case may be filed against the department for delay or rejection, according to this circular. Students should still check whether such a bar is lawful.
