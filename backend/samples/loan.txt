MSME WORKING CAPITAL LOAN TERMS (sample — no personal details)

1. Loan
A working capital loan may be given to a small business. The exact amount is written in a sanction letter, not in this sample.

2. Interest and charges
Interest is floating and can be changed by the lender at any time. A processing fee of 3% is deducted up front. A late payment attracts 3% per month extra, plus GST.

3. Security
The borrower must pledge business stock, machines, and personal guarantee of the owners. The lender may also mark a lien on a home, even if the home is a family residence.

4. Recall
The lender may demand the full loan back with 7 days notice, even if all EMIs are paid on time, if the lender feels the risk has increased.

5. Default
If one EMI is late by 15 days, the account may be treated as default. The lender may take possession of pledged goods without going to court first.

6. Insurance
The borrower must buy insurance from a company named by the lender. Failure to buy it is treated as default.

7. Information
The borrower must share all bank statements, GST filings, and partner details whenever asked. Refusal is default.

8. MSME payment rules
These terms say the buyer may pay vendors in 90 days. Statutory interest under the MSMED Act is waived by the borrower.

9. Courts
Disputes will be heard only in the city chosen by the lender.
