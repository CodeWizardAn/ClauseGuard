RENTAL AGREEMENT (sample — no personal details)

1. Parties
This agreement is between the owner of the home (the landlord) and the person living in the home (the tenant). Names and phone numbers are not part of this sample.

2. Rent and late fee
The tenant must pay monthly rent in advance by the 5th of every month. If rent is late by more than 3 days, a late fee of 5% of the monthly rent is added. The landlord may increase rent by up to 15% after 11 months with 30 days written notice.

3. Security deposit
The tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to three months of rent. The landlord may keep all or part of the deposit for damage beyond normal wear, unpaid rent, or painting. The landlord is not required to pay interest on the deposit. The deposit may be returned within 90 days after the tenant leaves.

4. Lock-in and exit
The tenant cannot leave for the first 11 months. If the tenant leaves early, the full security deposit is forfeited and two extra months of rent become payable immediately. The landlord may end this agreement with 15 days notice for any reason.

5. Entry and privacy
The landlord may enter the home at any time without notice to inspect, show the property, or carry out repairs.

6. Repairs
The tenant must pay for all repairs, including plumbing, electrical faults, and structural issues, even if they are not caused by the tenant.

7. Use of the home
The tenant shall not keep guests overnight for more than 2 nights without written permission. Sub-letting is not allowed. Pets are not allowed.

8. Dispute
Any dispute shall be decided only in a court chosen by the landlord. The tenant agrees not to approach consumer forums.

9. Changes
The landlord may change house rules at any time by sending a message. Continued stay means the tenant accepts the new rules.
